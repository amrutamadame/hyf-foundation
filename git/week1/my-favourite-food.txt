Tomato Soup 

Ingredients
3.5 pounds fresh tomatoes
1 head of garlic, peeled and smashed (about 6 cloves)
1 white or yellow onion, roughly chopped
3 tablespoons olive oil
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon black pepper
2 cups vegetable broth
1 large handful of fresh basil leaves
1/4 cup heavy cream (optional, for creaminess)
1 teaspoon sugar (optional, to balance acidity if needed) 


Instructions
Preheat and Roast: 
Heat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Place the tomatoes, smashed garlic, and chopped onion on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. 
Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with salt and pepper. 
Roast for about 50 to 60 minutes until the vegetables are soft, caramelized, and slightly charred.

Simmer with Broth: 
Transfer the roasted vegetables and all their juices into a large pot or Dutch oven. 
Add the vegetable or chicken broth and fresh basil leaves. 
Simmer over medium heat for about 5 to 10 minutes to let the flavors infuse.

Blend Until Smooth: 
Use an immersion blender right in the pot to blend the mixture until completely smooth and creamy.

Finish and Serve: 
Stir in a splash of heavy cream and taste the soup. 
Add a pinch of sugar if it needs acidity balance, or extra salt and pepper to taste. 
Serve warm with fresh basil, parmesan cheese, or crusty bread.
